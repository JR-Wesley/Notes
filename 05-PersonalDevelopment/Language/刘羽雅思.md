# Lesson 1

## 积累

- at large 逍遥法外；在逃
- come into (the zoo/research center) 传入；收到
- be spotted 被发现
- be taken seriously 被认真对待
- as evidence accumulates 随着证据增加
- feel obliged to do 不得不做；有义务做
- claim to have done 声称做过某事
- pick blackberries 采摘黑莓
- be cornered 被逼入绝境
- leave behind a trail of… 留下一串…… 痕迹
- complain of 抱怨（有某种声音 / 疼痛）
- be in the possession of sb 为某人所拥有
- manage to escape 成功逃脱
- it is disturbing to think that… 一想到…… 就令人不安
